Our Heritage

More Than Just Coffee.

"Born from a love for slow mornings, European piazza culture, and the perfect crema."

Nestled within the city’s quietest stone courtyard, The Cozy Cup is a tribute to the storied grand cafés of Vienna, Budapest, and Milan. We hand-select high-altitude heirloom beans, roasting them in micro-batches to amplify floral undertones and deep chocolate finishes.

Paired alongside classical viennoiserie laminated fresh before dawn with cultured organic butter, every detail is orchestrated to offer an unhurried, restorative sanctuary.
