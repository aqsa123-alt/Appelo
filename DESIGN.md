## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory warmth, quiet prestige, and artisanal intimacy of a modern European boutique café. It merges classic Viennese coffeehouse sophistication with contemporary Parisian minimalism, evoking the aroma of roasted espresso beans, handcrafted porcelain, and tailored walnut fixtures.

The visual style blends **Tactile / Warm Luxury** with **Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Target Audience:** Connoisseurs of specialty coffee, pastry enthusiasts, patrons seeking an unhurried sanctuary, and individuals who appreciate bespoke hospitality and curated culinary rituals.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, grounded, reassuringly opulent, slow-paced, and sensuous.
- **Design Philosophy:** Soft tactile feedback, spacious layouts that refuse to rush the eye, warm monochromatic layering, and delicate editorial typography that conveys refined craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the rich tonalities of artisanal coffee roasting, patisserie glazes, and botanical greenhouse courtyards:

- **Primary (`#2B1E16` Deep Roast Espresso):** Used for commanding typography, primary structural frames, icon accents, and high-emphasis controls. Supports a secondary roast shade (`#3D2B1F`) for states and subtle contrast.
- **Secondary (`#C68B59` Warm Caramel):** Serves as the tactile golden thread throughout the interface. Applied to interactive highlights, primary button backgrounds, badges, and accents. Accompanied by `#D99B66` for active/hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#5C6B50` Muted Olive Leaf):** A calm herbal tone representing tea leaves, botanical interiors, and culinary balance. Employed for success tokens, organic/seasonal highlights, and secondary tags. Paired with `#738363` for soft surface tints.
- **Neutral (`#FBF7F2` Velvety Cream Base):** The non-sterile canvas foundation. Supported by layered tints: `#F4EBE1` (Warm Beige / Surface Container) and `#EDE0D0` (Toasted Almond / Surface High / Low-contrast Borders).
- **Text Hierarchies:** Heading and primary text use `#2B1E16`; secondary body and supporting metadata use `#6E5D53` (Smoked Umber); disabled states use `#B3A59B`.

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clear, humanist legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Playfair Display introduces literary restraint, classic menu elegance, and tactile prestige. It is reserved for section dividers, culinary item names, feature callouts, and greeting banners.
- **Body & Numerical Information:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides roundness and warmth that mirrors the physical curve of porcelain cups and steam ribbons. Its high legibility ensures price tags, ingredients, origin tasting notes, and nutritional details remain effortlessly readable.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Label styles utilize slight uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to echo high-end European restaurant menus and boutique packaging stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an unhurried, editorial reading cadence with balanced white space that feels like browsing an artful magazine or salon menu.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column responsive layout with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container is capped at 1280px to preserve intimate line lengths.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm: 1.25rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component spacing defaults to generous internal breathing room. Cards and dialogue sheets favor `space-lg` padding to avoid cramped density, reflecting European hospitality's emphasis on lingering and comfort.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp drop-shadows and industrial elevation levels in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Caramel-Warm Ambient Shadows**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FBF7F2` (Velvety Cream).
  - **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards, Menus):** `#F4EBE1` with a fine `1px` solid border in `#EDE0D0`.
  - **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals, Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` with warm-tinted ambient diffusion.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow Recipe:**
  - *Resting Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.03)`
  - *Raised / Hover Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.09), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(198, 139, 89, 0.12)`
  - *Floating Elevation (Overlays, Modals):* `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.14)`
- **Border Philosophy:** Delicate, low-contrast borders (`#EDE0D0` or `rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.08)`) create structure without visual clutter, mirroring fine linen stitch work.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects organic ceramic contours, curved bentwood thonet chairs, and smooth river stones:

- **Interactive Elements & Standard Cards:** Defined at `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius).
- **Large Content Containers & Modals:** Styled at `rounded-lg` (1rem radius) to maintain a soft, welcoming demeanor.
- **Pill Badges & Circular Accents:** Rounded-full tokens applied to origin labels, order status chips, and circular icon buttons (e.g., adding a shot of espresso).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Rich Espresso `#2B1E16`, text in Velvety Cream `#FBF7F2`. Hover transition shifts smoothly to Caramel `#C68B59` with a subtle `translateY(-1px)` lift. Radius is `0.5rem`; padding is `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary / Soft Caramel:** Background in `#F4EBE1`, border `1px solid #EDE0D0`, text `#2B1E16`. Hover brings a `#C68B59` border and light ambient glow.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#2B1E16` text and subtle underline animation that expands from center outwards on hover.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- **Editorial Product Card:** Layered on `#F4EBE1` with a `1px solid #EDE0D0` border. Imagery utilizes rounded corners (`0.75rem`) within a `1rem` inner card padding. Price tags appear in Playfair Display (`title-lg`), accompanied by tasting notes in muted olive `#5C6B50`.
- **Tasting / Origin Banner:** Soft beige container accented with a thin vertical `#5C6B50` olive left rule and italicized Playfair headline.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Neutral canvas `#FFFFFF` on `#F4EBE1` backgrounds, wrapped in a `1px solid #EDE0D0` border. On focus, the border transitions to `#C68B59` accompanied by a soft caramel halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 139, 89, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#B3A59B`.
- **Quantity Selector:** Stepper buttons enclosed in an organic pill container with warm beige background `#EDE0D0` and espresso icons.

### Chips & Filters
- **Dietary & Tasting Chips (e.g., "Single Origin", "Oat Milk", "House Roast"):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.375rem 0.875rem`. Default state uses `#F4EBE1` surface with `#6E5D53` text. Active state uses `#2B1E16` with `#FBF7F2` text, or `#5C6B50` for botanical/organic selections.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons:** Custom circular rings with `#C68B59` active borders and a caramel inner dot.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded square (`0.25rem` radius), unchecked border in `#EDE0D0`, checking animates a warm fill of `#2B1E16` with a cream checkmark.

### Bespoke Café Components
- **Roast Level Slider:** A nuanced track shifting from light caramel (`#D99B66`) to dark roast espresso (`#2B1E16`), with a polished brass-toned circular thumb.
- **Table Reservation Accordion:** Warm beige folded card panels with delicate dotted separation lines mimicking vintage European receipt stationery.
